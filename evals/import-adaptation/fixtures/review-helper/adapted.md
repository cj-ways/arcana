# Review Helper

## Gotchas

1. Do not add checklist items that have no link to the actual review target.

## Steps

1. Read the task and identify the highest-risk review areas.
2. Build a short checklist tied to those areas.
3. Gather local evidence before escalating any concern.

## Validation

Remove checklist items that do not change the final review outcome.
