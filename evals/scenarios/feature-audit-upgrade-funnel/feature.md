# Subscription Upgrade Funnel

## Current Flow

Users land on a pricing table after their 14-day trial expires. They see three plans, a short list of features, and a billing form.

## Known Problems

- Trial-to-paid conversion dropped from 18% to 11% over the last quarter.
- Mobile users abandon the billing step at twice the desktop rate.
- User interviews say the differences between Pro and Scale are unclear.
- Customers do not trust the annual discount because the cancellation language is buried in the FAQ.

## Constraints

- Pricing is fixed for the next two months.
- Engineering can support copy, layout, instrumentation, and a single new comparison component.
