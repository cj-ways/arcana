# Idea Brief

Build a B2B analytics workspace for finance teams at mid-market SaaS companies.

## Core Users

- Revenue operations managers
- FP&A analysts
- Controllers who reconcile subscription metrics with accounting systems

## Core Jobs

- Explain revenue movement every week without exporting CSVs into spreadsheets
- Compare pipeline, bookings, and recognized revenue in one workspace
- Share board-ready charts with comments

## Constraints

- Founder wants an AI-friendly stack and prefers free-tier services at the start.
- The first release should support 5-10 pilot customers, not enterprise scale.
- Security matters because data includes customer ARR and billing history.
